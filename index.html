📖 How to play

  • Tap an empty cell to start a wall. It grows both ways at once — a red half and a blue half — until each reaches something solid.
  • The switch under the chamber sets which way a tap builds. Or swipe across or up–down from where you want the wall. On a mouse, right click or Space turns it.
  • Any region left with no particle in it is sealed and counts as claimed. Claim 75% to clear the level.
  • If a particle touches a half while it is still growing, that half is destroyed and you lose a life. The other half keeps going.
  • One wall at a time. Run out of lives or time and the run is over.
  • Level n has n+1 particles and n+1 lives.

Scoring

  • Every cell you claim is worth the level number.
  • Clearing a level pays, again times the level: 100 per life left, 5 per second left, and 60 for every percent you claim past 75.
